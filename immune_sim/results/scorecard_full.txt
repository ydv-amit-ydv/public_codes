====================================================================================================
HOW TO READ THIS
====================================================================================================
  Grade A is the only grade where a number was tested out-of-sample
  and could have failed. Grade C 'robustness' is the share of
  plausible parameter sets giving the stated direction - it measures
  how load-bearing a parameter guess is, NOT whether the biology is
  true. Grade D items are not claims at all; they are listed so
  nobody mistakes silence for evidence.
